Our story

Coffee, the slow way.

The Eritrean coffee ceremony is about more than caffeine: green beans are roasted right in front of you, ground by hand, and brewed in a clay jebena, then poured in three unhurried rounds while conversation happens around the table. That's the pace we keep at Urban Plaza Cafe.

Alongside the ceremony, our kitchen turns out injera platters, shiro, kitfo with tibsi, and fresh pastries, so there's always a reason to stay for a second cup.
